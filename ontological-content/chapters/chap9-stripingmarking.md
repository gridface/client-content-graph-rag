## **9. STRIPING, MARKING, AND ACCESSIBILITY**

### 9.1 Pavement Marking Standards
- **Material Types**
  - Traffic paint
  - Thermoplastic
  - Preformed tape
  - Epoxy systems
- **Color Standards and Applications**[107]
  - White: lane lines, edges, crosswalks
  - Yellow: centerlines, no-passing zones[107]
  - Blue: accessible parking
  - Red/green: special regulations
- **Line Widths and Dimensions**[104][107]
  - Standard 4-inch width[104][107]
  - 6-inch for emphasis areas
  - Edge line specifications
  - Double line spacing

### 9.2 Parking Lot Striping
- **Layout Design**[104]
  - Stall dimensions (9'×18' typical)
  - Aisle widths based on angle
  - Traffic flow patterns
  - Fire lanes and emergency access
- **Accessible Parking Requirements**[48]
  - ADA compliance[48]
  - Van-accessible spaces
  - Access aisles
  - Signage requirements
- **Application Timing**[107]
  - New asphalt: minimum 30 days cure[107]
  - Surface preparation
  - Weather conditions for application

### 9.3 Roadway Markings
- **Longitudinal Markings**
  - Centerlines
  - Lane lines
  - Edge lines
  - Turn lanes
- **Transverse Markings**
  - Stop bars
  - Crosswalks
  - Yield lines
  - Speed legends
- **Reflectivity Requirements**
  - Nighttime visibility standards
  - Retroreflectivity measurements
  - Maintenance schedules
