## Notes on Using These Resources

1. **Local Specifications**: Always verify local requirements and specifications. State DOTs, municipalities, and project specifications take precedence over general industry guidelines.

2. **Standards Access**: ASTM, AASHTO, and some other standards require purchase or membership for full access. Many state DOTs, universities, and technical libraries provide access.

3. **Seasonal Considerations**: Repair techniques and materials may vary by season and climate. Consult resources specific to your geographic region and current weather conditions.

4. **Material Selection**: Work with your suppliers for specific product recommendations, application rates, and technical support for repair materials.

5. **Training Investment**: Invest in training for distress identification and repair techniques. Knowledgeable crews deliver better quality repairs and reduce callbacks.

6. **Documentation**: Document conditions before, during, and after repairs. Photos, measurements, and written descriptions protect you and provide valuable records for future reference.

7. **Warranty Considerations**: Understand the difference between construction defects, design deficiencies, and normal wear. Clear documentation helps resolve warranty questions.
