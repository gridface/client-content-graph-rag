## **2. ASPHALT MIX TYPES AND TECHNOLOGIES**

### 2.1 Hot Mix Asphalt (HMA)
- **Production temperatures and requirements**[23][29][32]
  - Mixing: 300-350°F (150-175°C)
  - Laying: 220-290°F (104-143°C)
  - Compaction: minimum 185°F (85°C)
- **Dense-Graded Mixes**[19]
  - Coarse-graded mixes
  - Fine-graded mixes
  - Applications and performance
- **Stone Matrix Asphalt (SMA)**[5][19]
  - Gap-graded aggregate structure
  - High stone content
  - Fiber stabilizers
  - Rutting resistance
- **Open-Graded Friction Course (OGFC)**[19]
  - Drainage characteristics
  - Noise reduction properties
  - Permeability requirements

### 2.2 Warm Mix Asphalt (WMA)
- **Technology overview and benefits**[100][103][106]
  - Production temperatures: 215-275°F (102-135°C)
  - Energy savings (20% fuel reduction)[118]
  - Emissions reduction
  - Extended haul distances
  - Cold weather paving capabilities[109][115]
- **WMA Technologies**[106][112]
  - Foaming technologies (water-based)
  - Organic additives (waxes)
  - Chemical additives
  - Process modifications
- **Applications and performance considerations**
  - Increased RAP utilization potential[103][109]
  - Compaction advantages
  - Durability and aging characteristics

### 2.3 Cold Mix Asphalt (CMA)
- **Asphalt Emulsions**[3]
  - Cationic vs. anionic emulsions
  - Breaking speed classifications (fast/medium/slow)
  - Applications in surface treatments and repairs
- **Cutback Asphalts**[3]
  - Rapid, medium, and slow-curing cutbacks
  - VOC concerns and restrictions
  - Limited modern applications

### 2.4 Specialty Asphalt Mixes
- **Porous Asphalt**
  - Stormwater management applications[1]
  - Void content and permeability
  - Maintenance considerations
- **Rubberized Asphalt (Crumb Rubber Modified)**
  - Tire rubber incorporation
  - Performance benefits
  - Noise reduction
- **High-Performance Mixes**
  - Heavy-duty applications[5]
  - Large-stone mixtures (1.5-2 inch NMAS)
  - Industrial and airport pavements
