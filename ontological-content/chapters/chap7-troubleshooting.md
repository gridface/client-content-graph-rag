## **7. COMMON PAVEMENT DISTRESSES AND TROUBLESHOOTING**

### 7.1 Cracking Distresses

#### 7.1.1 Alligator (Fatigue) Cracking
- **Description**[61][67][70][73]
  - Interconnected cracks resembling alligator skin[61]
  - Series of small blocks or polygons
  - Occurs in wheel paths
- **Causes**[61][70]
  - Structural failure of pavement layers[73]
  - Repeated traffic loading (fatigue)
  - Inadequate pavement thickness
  - Weak subgrade or base
  - Poor drainage[61]
- **Repair Solutions**[61][70][73]
  - Early stage: crack sealing and seal coat[70]
  - Advanced stage: full-depth patching required[61][73]
  - Mill and overlay for larger areas
  - Address underlying structural issues[70]

#### 7.1.2 Block Cracking
- **Description**[61][73]
  - Large interconnected rectangular cracks[73]
  - Parallel and perpendicular pattern
  - Not load-associated[61][73]
- **Causes**[73]
  - Asphalt shrinkage (thermal cycling)[61]
  - Aging and oxidation of binder[73]
  - Low penetration asphalt
  - Mix placed too dry[73]
- **Repair Solutions**[61][73]
  - Early: crack sealing and sealcoating[61]
  - Advanced: mill and overlay
  - Severe: full reconstruction[73]

#### 7.1.3 Edge Cracking
- **Description**[61][64][67][73]
  - Cracks along pavement edges[61][67]
  - Parallel to pavement edge
- **Causes**[61][64][67]
  - Poor drainage[61][64]
  - Lack of lateral support
  - Vegetation encroachment[67][73]
  - Settlement of underlying layers
- **Repair Solutions**[61][67][73]
  - Remove vegetation[67][73]
  - Correct drainage problems[64][67][73]
  - Crack seal/fill[73]
  - Full-depth patch for severe cases[61][73]

#### 7.1.4 Longitudinal and Transverse Cracking
- **Description**
  - Linear cracks parallel or perpendicular to centerline
  - May be thermal or reflective in nature
- **Causes**
  - Thermal stress (low temperature)
  - Paving joint failures
  - Reflection from underlying cracks
  - Inadequate joint construction
- **Repair Solutions**
  - Crack sealing
  - Rout and seal for wider cracks
  - Overlay with crack relief interlayer

#### 7.1.5 Joint Reflection Cracking
- **Description**[61][64][73]
  - Cracks directly over underlying rigid pavement joints[73]
  - Asphalt overlay over concrete pavement
- **Causes**[61][64]
  - Thermal expansion/contraction[64]
  - Traffic loading[64]
  - Vertical movement of concrete slabs
- **Repair Solutions**[61][64][73]
  - Remove and replace affected area[64]
  - Mill and overlay with stress-absorbing membrane
  - Crack relief systems

#### 7.1.6 Slippage Cracking
- **Description**[67]
  - Crescent-shaped cracks[67]
  - "Stretch mark" appearance[67]
- **Causes**[67]
  - Poor bond between asphalt layers[67]
  - Lack of tack coat[67]
  - Contamination (dirt, oil) preventing adhesion[67]
- **Repair Solutions**[67]
  - Full-depth removal to below slippage source[67]
  - Proper tack coat application in new work
  - Clean surface preparation

### 7.2 Surface Deformation Distresses

#### 7.2.1 Rutting
- **Description**[61][64][67][76]
  - Longitudinal depressions in wheel paths[67][76]
  - Surface depression from traffic loads
- **Causes**[64][67][76]
  - Consolidation or lateral movement of materials[76]
  - Inadequate compaction[64][67]
  - Weak asphalt mix[76]
  - High temperatures and heavy loads
  - Subgrade/base inadequacy[76]
- **Repair Solutions**[61][64][67]
  - Minor: overlay or mill and resurface[64][79]
  - Moderate: mill affected lanes and repave[67]
  - Severe: remove and replace with proper mix
  - Address drainage if moisture-related[64]

#### 7.2.2 Corrugation and Shoving
- **Description**[67]
  - Ripples (corrugation) or waves (shoving)[67]
  - Perpendicular to traffic direction
- **Causes**[67][76]
  - Unstable asphalt layer[67][76]
  - Poor mix design or manufacturing[67]
  - Frequent traffic start/stop locations[67]
  - High horizontal shear stresses
- **Repair Solutions**[67]
  - Partial to full-depth patching[67]
  - Remove unstable material
  - Replace with proper mix design

#### 7.2.3 Depressions (Birdbaths)
- **Description**[67][70]
  - Localized low areas that collect water[67]
  - Standing water after rainfall
- **Causes**[70]
  - Settlement of base or subgrade
  - Poor initial compaction
  - Inadequate design
- **Repair Solutions**[70]
  - Temporary: surface patching[70]
  - Permanent: remove and replace with proper subgrade repair[70]

### 7.3 Disintegration Distresses

#### 7.3.1 Raveling (Weathering)
- **Description**[67][70]
  - Loss of aggregate particles from surface[67]
  - Progressive deterioration from surface downward[79]
  - Rough, pockmarked texture[79]
- **Causes**[67][70]
  - Oxidation and aging[67]
  - Poor compaction[67]
  - Low-quality mix (insufficient binder)[67][70]
  - Inadequate curing
  - Freeze-thaw damage
- **Repair Solutions**[67][70]
  - Early: sealcoating[67][70]
  - Moderate: thin overlay[67]
  - Severe: mill and resurface or full-depth patch[67][70]

#### 7.3.2 Potholes
- **Description**[61]
  - Bowl-shaped holes in pavement surface
  - Complete pavement disintegration in localized area
- **Causes**[61]
  - Water infiltration through cracks[61]
  - Freeze-thaw cycles
  - Traffic weakening of compromised area
  - Poor drainage[61]
- **Repair Solutions**[61]
  - Remove loose material and debris
  - Tack coat application
  - Hot mix asphalt patching[61]
  - Proper compaction
  - Full-depth repair preferred

### 7.4 Drainage-Related Issues
- **Poor Drainage Problems**[64]
  - Standing water/ponding
  - Accelerated pavement deterioration
  - Base saturation and weakening
- **Solutions**[64]
  - Improve drainage systems[64]
  - Install or repair inlets and pipes[64]
  - Reshape surface for positive drainage[64]
  - Underdrains for subsurface water

### 7.5 Pavement Evaluation and Assessment
- **Visual Inspection Methods**
  - Distress identification and mapping
  - Severity rating systems
  - Extent quantification
- **Pavement Condition Index (PCI)**
  - Numerical rating (0-100)
  - Standardized evaluation procedure
  - Maintenance priority determination
- **Structural Evaluation**
  - Falling Weight Deflectometer (FWD) testing
  - Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR)
  - Coring and layer evaluation
  - Remaining life estimation
