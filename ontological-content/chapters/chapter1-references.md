## Notes on Using These Resources

1. **Standards Access**: AASHTO and ASTM standards require purchase or membership for full access. Many state DOTs and public libraries provide access to these standards.

2. **State Specifications**: Always verify local requirements. State and municipal specifications take precedence over general industry guidelines.

3. **Currency**: Standards and specifications are updated regularly. Always verify you're using the current version.

4. **Technical Support**: Most organizations listed offer technical support hotlines or email support for members or the public.

5. **Training and Certification**: Consider pursuing professional certifications through NAPA (APT), Asphalt Institute, or state DOT programs to enhance credibility and knowledge.

## Citation Format Used in Chapter 1

The chapter content was developed using established industry knowledge and best practices from these authoritative sources. Specific technical data points (e.g., temperature ranges, mixture proportions, testing standards) are based on:

- ASTM and AASHTO test methods and specifications
- Asphalt Institute technical manuals (MS-1, MS-2, MS-22, MS-26)
- NAPA Quality Improvement Publications
- FHWA technical guidance documents
- State DOT standard specifications
- Current industry best practices as documented by professional organizations

Where specific numerical values or technical procedures are cited, they reflect consensus industry standards as published in the referenced materials.
