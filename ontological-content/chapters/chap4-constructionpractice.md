### 4.6 Weather and Seasonal Considerations

#### 4.6.1 Temperature Requirements
- **Ambient Air Temperature**[23][26][29][35]
  - Minimum: 50°F (10°C) and rising[23][26]
  - Cold weather paving: 32-50°F with special provisions[38]
  - High wind restrictions
- **Ground Temperature**[23][32]
  - Minimum: 50°F (10°C)[23]
  - Infrared thermometer measurements
  - Early morning concerns
- **Mix Temperature Management**[23][26][29][32]
  - Delivery: above 260°F (127°C)[32]
  - Laying: 220-290°F (104-143°C)[23][29]
  - Final compaction: above 185°F (85°C)[23][26]

#### 4.6.2 Cold Weather Paving
- **Challenges and Solutions**[26][35][38]
  - Rapid cooling rates
  - Compaction time limitations (16 minutes for thin lifts)[26]
  - WMA technology for season extension[103][109][115]
  - Increased lift thickness requirements
- **Special Procedures**[38][115]
  - Cold weather paving plans
  - Contractor quality control requirements
  - Temperature monitoring protocols

#### 4.6.3 Hot Weather Paving
- **Considerations**[35]
  - Mat overheating prevention
  - Mix temperature control
  - Tender mix issues
  - Adequate compaction timing

#### 4.6.4 Wet Weather Restrictions
- **Limitations**[29][35]
  - No paving during active precipitation
  - Moisture effects on compaction
  - Cooling acceleration from evaporation
  - Base saturation concerns
