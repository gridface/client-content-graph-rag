## Notes on Using These Resources

1. **Equipment Selection**: Always evaluate equipment based on your typical project types, sizes, and frequency of use. Consider rental vs. purchase for specialized equipment.

2. **Manufacturer Support**: Dealer network quality, parts availability, and service support should factor into equipment purchase decisions as much as initial cost.

3. **Operator Training**: Invest in proper operator training. Well-trained operators are more productive, cause less equipment damage, and produce higher quality work.

4. **Maintenance**: Follow manufacturer maintenance schedules. Preventive maintenance extends equipment life and reduces downtime.

5. **Safety Compliance**: Ensure all equipment meets OSHA requirements and operators are properly trained and certified where required.

6. **Technology Updates**: Equipment technology evolves rapidly. Grade control, GPS guidance, and telematics systems can significantly improve productivity and quality—evaluate ROI for your operations.

7. **Regional Considerations**: Climate, project types, and local material characteristics affect equipment selection. Consult with regional dealers and associations for location-specific guidance.

8. **Specification Compliance**: For public works and DOT projects, verify that your equipment meets specification requirements. Some specs require specific brands or capabilities.
