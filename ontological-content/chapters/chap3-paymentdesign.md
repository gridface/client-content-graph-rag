### 3.3 Thickness Design
- **Structural Number (SN) Concept**[80][83]
  - Formula: SN = a₁D₁ + a₂D₂M₂ + a₃D₃M₃
  - Layer coefficients (a-values)
  - Drainage coefficients (M-values)
  - Thickness determination
- **Traffic Analysis**[5][10]
  - Equivalent Single Axle Load (ESAL) calculations
  - Traffic volume projections
  - Heavy vehicle considerations
  - Loading conditions and stress concentrations
- **Design Considerations**
  - Full-depth asphalt vs. aggregate base designs[7]
  - Minimum lift thickness requirements[2][89]
  - 3-4× nominal maximum aggregate size (NMAS) rule

### 3.4 Climate and Environmental Design Factors
- **Temperature Effects**
  - PG binder selection for climate[82][85]
  - Thermal stress and cracking
  - Freeze-thaw considerations
- **Moisture Management**
  - Drainage design requirements[7][104][107]
  - Minimum slopes (typically 2% or 1/4 inch per foot)
  - Subsurface drainage systems
  - Moisture sensitivity of materials

### 3.5 Application-Specific Design

#### 3.5.1 Commercial Asphalt Pavement Design
- **Parking Lots**[42][104][107][113]
  - Traffic volume and loading analysis
  - Pavement thickness: typically 4-6 inches[48][51]
  - Layout and circulation patterns
  - Drainage provisions and slope requirements
  - ADA compliance requirements[48]
- **Loading Docks and Heavy-Use Areas**
  - Increased structural requirements
  - Heavy vehicle considerations
  - Turning and braking zones

#### 3.5.2 Residential Asphalt Pavement Design
- **Driveways**[42][48][51]
  - Thickness: typically 2-3 inches[48][51]
  - Light vehicle loading
  - Base requirements (4-6 inch compacted base)[51]
  - Aesthetic considerations
- **Private Roads and Subdivisions**
  - Collector and local street design
  - Lower traffic volumes
  - Cost-effective solutions

#### 3.5.3 Municipal and Public Works Design
- **City Streets and Roadways**[42]
  - Functional classification systems
  - Public safety requirements
  - Utility coordination
  - Traffic management during construction
- **Bike Paths and Multi-Use Trails**[1]
  - Reduced structural requirements
  - Smooth surface specifications
  - Width and geometric standards

#### 3.5.4 Industrial and Heavy-Duty Pavement Design
- **Airports and Runways**[81][84][87]
  - FAA design standards (AC 150/5320-6)[81][90]
  - FAARFIELD software requirements
  - Heavy aircraft loading
  - High tire pressures
  - Surface friction requirements
  - Mix specifications (FAA P-401, P-403)[84][93]
- **Ports and Intermodal Facilities**[5]
  - Container handling equipment loads
  - Very heavy-duty requirements
  - Large-stone mixtures
