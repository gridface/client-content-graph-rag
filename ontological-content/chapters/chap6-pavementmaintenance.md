### 6.3 Crack Treatment Methods
- **Crack Sealing and Filling**[21][24][27][30]
  - **Clean and Seal**[24][30]
    - Air lance or compressed air cleaning
    - Hot-pour sealant application
    - Material types (rubberized asphalt, polymer-modified)
  - **Rout and Seal**[24][30]
    - Router equipment and dimensions
    - Reservoir creation
    - Enhanced sealant retention
  - **Saw and Seal**[30]
    - Precision cutting
    - Uniform dimensions
  - **Full-Depth Crack Repair**[24][30]
    - Severe cracking treatment
    - Material removal and replacement
- **Timing and Applications**[21]
  - Crack sealing: immediately upon appearance
  - Annual to biannual frequency
  - Temperature requirements for application

### 6.4 Surface Treatment Applications

#### 6.4.1 Sealcoating
- **Purpose and Benefits**[21][33][71]
  - UV protection and oxidation prevention[21]
  - Water infiltration reduction
  - Surface appearance enhancement
  - Extended pavement life (5-7 years added)[21]
- **Materials**[71]
  - Coal tar emulsions (environmental concerns noted)[71]
  - Asphalt emulsion-based sealers
  - Application rates and thickness
- **Application Procedures**[21][33]
  - Surface preparation and cleaning[21][33]
  - Optimal temperature conditions
  - Application frequency (every 2-3 years)[21][33]
  - Curing time requirements

#### 6.4.2 Fog Seal
- **Characteristics**[24][30]
  - Diluted asphalt emulsion application
  - Thin protective coating
  - Oxidation and raveling prevention
- **Applications**
  - Aged pavement surfaces
  - Minor surface weathering
  - Low-cost maintenance option

#### 6.4.3 Chip Seal
- **Construction Process**[62][65][68][71]
  - Asphalt binder application (distributor truck)[65]
  - Aggregate spreading (mechanical spreader)[65]
  - Pneumatic roller compaction[65]
  - Loose aggregate removal (rotary broom)[65]
- **Materials and Specifications**[65]
  - Binder types (emulsified, PG binders, modified)[65]
  - Aggregate size (typically ¼ to ½ inch)[65]
  - Aggregate quality (crushed stone, gravel, slag)[65]
  - Application rates
- **Design Considerations**[65][68][71]
  - Traffic volume and type
  - Climate conditions
  - Single, double, or triple applications[65]
  - Cape seal combinations (chip seal + slurry/micro)
- **Performance**[62][71]
  - Skid resistance restoration[65]
  - Surface sealing from moisture[65]
  - Textured finish[62]
  - Cost-effectiveness[65]
  - Lifespan expectations[62]

#### 6.4.4 Slurry Seal
- **Composition**[62][68][71][74]
  - Asphalt emulsion[68][74]
  - Fine aggregate (well-graded)[68]
  - Mineral filler[68][74]
  - Water and additives[68][74]
  - Polymer modifications (common)[68]
- **Mix Types**[71][74]
  - Type I: Fine aggregate (low traffic)[71]
  - Type II: Medium aggregate (raveling repair)[71]
  - Type III: Coarse aggregate (severe imperfections)[71]
- **Application**[62][68][71][74]
  - Self-contained continuous mixer[74]
  - Squeegee spreading device[77]
  - Thickness: 3-9 mm (1/8 to 3/8 inch)[74]
  - Smooth finish characteristics[62][77]
- **Benefits and Uses**[62][68][71]
  - Fill minor cracks and surface imperfections[71][77]
  - Water infiltration prevention[71]
  - Skid resistance enhancement[77]
  - Residential street applications[68]
  - Parking lot preservation
  - Lifespan: approximately 5-7 years[71]

#### 6.4.5 Microsurfacing
- **Characteristics**
  - Similar to slurry seal with polymer-modified emulsion
  - Quick-setting properties
  - Thicker application than slurry seal
  - Can correct minor rutting and surface deformations
- **Applications**
  - High-traffic areas
  - Rut filling
  - Surface course replacement

### 6.5 Structural Repair Methods

#### 6.5.1 Patching and Pothole Repair
- **Hot Mix Asphalt Patching**[21][24][27][39]
  - **Remove and Replace (Full-Depth)**[27][39]
    - Damaged area removal
    - Square or rectangular cuts
    - Tack coat application[39]
    - HMA placement and compaction
    - Permanent repair solution[27]
  - **Surface (Skin) Patching**[27]
    - Shallow repairs
    - Less durable than full-depth[27]
    - Temporary to semi-permanent
- **Cold Mix Asphalt**[24]
  - Emergency and temporary repairs
  - Winter maintenance applications
  - Stockpile material
  - Lower durability
- **Spray Injection Patching**[24]
  - Specialized equipment
  - Rapid repair method
  - Emulsion and aggregate application
  - Moderate durability

#### 6.5.2 Milling (Cold Planing) and Resurfacing
- **Milling Operations**[27][34]
  - **Cold Planing Process**
    - Rotating drum with cutting teeth[34]
    - Precision depth control
    - Material removal and loading
  - **Applications**[27]
    - Surface profile correction
    - Thickness adjustment for elevation matching
    - Removal of distressed surface layers
    - Preparation for overlay
- **Resurfacing (Overlay)**[21][27][33][36]
  - New asphalt layer placement over existing pavement
  - Thickness typically 1.5-4 inches
  - Surface restoration and strengthening
  - Extends pavement life (10+ years)[33]
- **Mill and Overlay**[27][36]
  - Combined milling and resurfacing operation
  - Most common rehabilitation technique
  - Maintains existing elevations
  - Addresses surface distress

#### 6.5.3 Advanced Rehabilitation Techniques
- **Asphalt Overlay (Without Milling)**[21][36]
  - Structural strengthening
  - Increased pavement thickness
  - Minor surface defect repair required first
  - Elevation changes considerations
- **Full-Depth Reclamation (FDR)**[36]
  - In-place pulverization of existing pavement
  - Mixing with base materials
  - Stabilization with cement, lime, or emulsion
  - New asphalt surface placement
  - Cost-effective reconstruction alternative
- **Cold In-Place Recycling (CIR)**
  - Existing asphalt milling and crushing
  - Emulsion or foamed asphalt addition
  - Re-laying recycled material
  - Environmental benefits
- **Hot In-Place Recycling (HIR)**
  - Surface heating and scarification
  - Remixing with rejuvenators
  - Re-laying and compaction
  - Minimal disruption

### 6.6 Maintenance Schedule Guidelines

#### 6.6.1 Commercial Parking Lot Maintenance[21]
- **Year 1-2**: Monitoring, inspections, minor crack sealing[21]
- **Year 3-5**: First sealcoating, crack filling[21]
- **Year 6-10**: Regular sealcoating (every 2-3 years), patching[21]
- **Year 10-15**: Overlay or major rehabilitation[21][33]
- **Year 15-20**: Potential reconstruction evaluation

#### 6.6.2 Residential Driveway Maintenance[21]
- **Annual**: Inspection, cleaning, minor crack repair[21]
- **Every 2-3 years**: Sealcoating application[21]
- **As needed**: Patching and localized repairs
- **Every 10-15 years**: Overlay or reconstruction[33]

#### 6.6.3 Municipal Roadway Maintenance
- **Annual**: Surface inspection, crack sealing
- **3-5 years**: Surface treatment (chip seal, slurry, micro)
- **10-15 years**: Mill and overlay
- **20-30 years**: Major rehabilitation or reconstruction
