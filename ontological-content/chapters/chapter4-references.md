## Conclusion

The resources listed in this chapter represent the most authoritative and widely-recognized sources for asphalt pavement construction practices and procedures. Contractors should maintain access to applicable state and local specifications, as requirements can vary significantly by jurisdiction. Regular review of updated standards and specifications is essential to ensure compliance with current industry practices.

For questions regarding specific applications or regional requirements, contractors should consult with:
- State or local agency engineers
- Mix design engineers
- Materials suppliers
- Industry associations
- Qualified testing laboratories

Staying current with evolving construction practices, equipment technology, and quality control methods will help contractors build better pavements, improve efficiency, and maintain competitive advantages in the marketplace.
