## Notes on Using These Resources

1. **Local Regulations**: Always verify compliance with state and local specifications. Maintenance standards and requirements vary by jurisdiction.

2. **Manufacturer Support**: Most equipment and material manufacturers provide excellent technical support, training, and troubleshooting assistance. Develop relationships with local suppliers.

3. **Technology Updates**: Maintenance technology and materials continuously improve. Stay current with industry publications, training opportunities, and manufacturer updates.

4. **Certification Value**: Professional certifications in maintenance techniques (crack sealing, slurry seal, etc.) differentiate your business and demonstrate expertise to customers.

5. **Safety Priority**: All maintenance operations involve safety considerations. Follow OSHA requirements, manufacturer guidelines, and industry best practices. Invest in proper training and equipment.

6. **Documentation**: Maintain detailed records of maintenance activities, dates, materials, costs, and results. This information is valuable for future planning and demonstrates professionalism to customers.

7. **Regional Expertise**: Climate, local materials, and regional practices significantly affect maintenance success. Resources from your geographic region and experienced local contractors are particularly valuable.

## Citation Format Used in Chapter 6

The chapter content was developed using established industry knowledge and best practices from these authoritative sources. Specific technical data points (e.g., application rates, timing guidelines, performance expectations, costs) are based on:

- Asphalt Institute technical manuals (MS-19, MS-22, MS-26, IS-115, IS-142, IS-178)
- NAPA Quality Improvement Publications and maintenance guidelines
- Foundation for Pavement Preservation (FP2) technical resources and best practices
- FHWA pavement preservation guidance and research
- International Slurry Surfacing Association (ISSA) specifications and guidelines
- ASTM and AASHTO test methods and material specifications
- Asphalt Recycling & Reclaiming Association (ARRA) technical documentation
- State DOT maintenance standards and specifications
- Industry best practices as documented by professional organizations
- Equipment manufacturer technical documentation and application guidelines

Where specific numerical values, application rates, performance data, or technical procedures are cited, they reflect consensus industry standards as published in the referenced materials and verified through multiple authoritative sources.

Maintenance economics and life-cycle cost data are based on published pavement management research, case studies from multiple agencies, and documented industry experience across various climate zones and traffic conditions.
